Brackenbury OS: research and build plan

The full business on one dashboard. Research from public records and sector sources, 21 Oct 2026.

The short version

Brackenbury is a 7-person, cash-rich bespoke furniture business that designs, project-manages and mostly subcontracts the making. That shapes the system: the biggest wins are pricing subcontract crafts quickly, chasing approvals automatically, and seeing every job's money and stage in one place. The prototype shows all 14 core screens with demo data. Supabase, Next.js and Xero can do everything here except one thing: there is no public material price API, so the price book has to learn from supplier quotes and invoices.

What is and is not possible
Xero accountingWorks. Invoices, bills, contacts, quotes, bank transactions, attachments. Limits 60 calls a minute and 5,000 a day; free Starter tier only 1,000 a day source
Xero payrollRead only for pay runs and payslips. Employees can be created and updated; pay runs cannot be posted through the API source
BanksPull bank data through Xero. The free GoCardless bank data service has closed to new sign-ups; TrueLayer and Yapily are paid and sales-led source
Material pricesNo public API from James Latham, Morgan Timber, Egger, Timbmet, Häfele or Blum. The price book is built from supplier quotes, invoices and Xero bills source
Price indexGovernment monthly building materials statistics include timber and joinery price indices, usable to flag price movement source
EmailMicrosoft Graph or Gmail API, or simply forward enquiries@ to an inbound parse address (Postmark or Resend) source
E-signatureValid in England and Wales for quote approvals; deeds still need witnessing source
RolesSupabase custom access token hook plus Row Level Security, so a workshop login can never read pay or margins source

Things you didn't ask for that will matter
1

The 2 weeks is probably waiting, not maths

With most making subcontracted, a quote waits on the gilder, the stone yard and the leather supplier. A subcontractor price-request link, with automatic chasing and a ballpark from past jobs, will save more time than faster arithmetic.

2

Quote against actual, every job

Log real hours and real bills against each job. After 20 jobs the estimator knows Brackenbury's true hours for veneer matching or a brass edge, and quotes get faster and safer.

3

Price age warnings

No UK timber merchant has a public price API. Every price carries its date and source, anything older than 90 days is flagged, and the government's monthly building materials index shows how much timber and board prices have moved.

4

QR labels on every part

The workshop question you raised (where does this piece go?) is answered by scanning the label: the 3D model opens with that part highlighted.

5

Compliance packs for yachts and jets

Commercial yachts need IMO FTP Code certificates per fabric and foam combination; aircraft parts need FAA/EASA 25.853 burn tests. The system assembles the pack per job so nothing goes missing at delivery.

6

VAT and export evidence

Private yachts and jets usually do not qualify for zero-rating as ships or aircraft. Zero-rating depends on proof of export, so the system stores export evidence per job.

7

CIS switch

Workshop manufacture is outside the Construction Industry Scheme; fitting on site for a contractor is inside it. A contract-type field sets CIS and payment-notice rules automatically.

8

Deposits as a number you watch

The 2025 accounts show about £448k of other creditors, very likely client deposits. Deposits taken against work still to do should be visible on the dashboard, job by job.

9

Several companies, one system

Mijato and Merrow share the address and the owners. Build multi-company in from day 1 so each has its own Xero file but one dashboard.

10

NDA mode

Projects under NDA show a codename on workshop screens and labels, with the real client name visible only to directors and the office.

11

Right to work clean-up

UK guidance says keep right-to-work copies for the job plus 2 years. The system schedules the deletion so you stay GDPR-safe.

12

Payroll cost feeds the quote

Hourly cost per person (salary plus employer NI and pension) comes from payroll data, so labour in every estimate is real cost, not a guess.

Ask your friend these 10 questions
  1. What turnover and how many jobs a year, and what is a typical job value?
  2. How are quotes built today (spreadsheet, Word, software), who does it, and how does the 2 weeks split between pricing materials and waiting on subcontractors?
  3. How much is made at Beacon Farm and how much by outside workshops?
  4. Which software is used now for accounts, CAD, email and project tracking? Is it Xero?
  5. Which suppliers do they buy board, timber, hardware and leather from?
  6. How are deposits and stage payments set, and in which currencies?
  7. Do they do on-site installation, and for whom (contractors, designers, clients)?
  8. Do they hold IMO FTP Code or 25.853 test certificates for marine and aviation work?
  9. Do Mijato and Merrow share staff, quoting and accounts with Brackenbury?
  10. Who would use the system day to day, and who needs to see pay?
